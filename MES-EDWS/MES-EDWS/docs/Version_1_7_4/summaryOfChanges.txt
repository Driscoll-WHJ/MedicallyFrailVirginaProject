Here’s a summary of the changes- 
- caseAction replaced by required individualCaseAction (reference table RT_INDIVIDUALCASEACTION)
- source and updateSw removed from AcknowledgementDTO, CepDWResultDTO and CepDWAckResponseDTO
- currentlyIncarcerated renamed incarceratedCircumstance
- individualComplyingWithTANFWorkParticipationRequirements replaced by enrolledForTANF
- MedicarePartABDTO: typeOfCoverage, medicareExpenseType, overallTypeOfCoverage and isSOLQ removed; replaced by required entitledToOrReceivingMedicareType
- PregnancyDTO replaced by PregnancyCircumstanceDTO
- SOLQ flags moved out of FrailtyDTO into SOLQTitleDisabledDTO (solqTitleDisability array); lTSSScreeningDetails array added (LTSSScreeningDTO); CBCTypeDetailsDTO replaced by indvCBCTypeDetails array (IndvCBCTypeDetailsDTO)

New required fields
- institutionCode and creditHours in EducationVerifiedDTO
- source and totalBudgetHours in EvIndvIncomeBudgetDTO
- maVerification made required
- Job training organizationName, monthlyHours and startDate made required

Non-breaking
- Made optional: inKindOrUnpaidWork, dependentDOB, effectiveBeginDate (caregiver and foster care), clientId in DocumentsDTO, several education and employment fields, several FrailtyDTO flags, circumstances
- documentUploadDate is now DateTime
- maDisabilityEndDate and SNAPExemptionType added
- Addition of SOLQTypeDisabledDTO and LTSSScreeningDTO
- CircumstanceArray DTO is now optional
- InKindorUnpaidWorkDTO is now optional
- Within EducationDTO, credit hours were added and monthlyhours, term end date, and verification source are now optional
- Within the electronicallyVerifiedEmploymentDTO, monthly hours is now optional
